Its 14 predictors are lags 1-12, 24 and 36; monthly cross-sectional OLS estimates are aggregated using Fama-MacBeth inference. CRSP inputs span 1926-1987, with estimation in 1929-1982.
The forecasting procedure on p.887 deliberately removes that future-dependent transformation. It uses raw-return regressions estimated over the previous 60 months, updates monthly, and forms equally weighted forecast deciles. January coefficients use prior January regressions. Trading evaluation covers 1934-1987. Page 888 uses the CRSP equal-weighted market, one-month Treasury bills and White heteroskedasticity-consistent inference.
